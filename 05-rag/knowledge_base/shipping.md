# Shipping Policy

Demo Store is a fictional online retailer used for the Level 5 RAG chatbot project.

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within **1–2 business days** after confirmation.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

Processing time is separate from shipping time.

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days** after dispatch.

Including order processing, most orders arrive approximately **4–7 business days** after confirmation.

Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.

## Shipping Costs

Standard shipping costs **$4.99** for orders with a merchandise subtotal below $50.

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of **$50 or more qualify for free standard shipping**.

The subtotal is calculated after discounts and before taxes.

All prices are in USD.

## Shipping Confirmation

Customers receive a shipping confirmation email when their order is dispatched.

The email contains a tracking link.

Customers can also find tracking information by visiting:

**Account → Orders → Track Package**

Tracking information may take up to **24 hours after dispatch** to become available.

## Shipping Delays

Shipping delays may occur because of:

- Severe weather conditions
- Carrier disruptions
- High order volumes
- Public holidays

If an order has not arrived **two business days after the estimated delivery date**, customers should contact customer support.

## Address Changes

Customers can request an address change before their order is dispatched.

Address changes are not guaranteed.

Once an order has been dispatched, the shipping address cannot be changed by Demo Store.

## International Shipping

International shipping options and express delivery services are not specified in the current store policies.

Customers should contact support to confirm availability.
