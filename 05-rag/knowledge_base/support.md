
# Customer Support Policy

Demo Store is a fictional online retailer created for the Level 5 RAG chatbot project.

The support contact information is illustrative and does not represent an operational customer service department.

## Contact Information

Customers can contact the fictional support team by email.

**Support Email:** support@demostore.example

The `.example` domain is reserved for demonstration purposes and is not a functional customer support address.

## Support Hours

Customer support operates:

**Monday–Friday: 09:00–17:00 UTC**

Support is unavailable on public holidays.

Customers may submit email requests outside business hours.

## Response Times

The support team aims to provide an initial response within **2 business days**.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

The response target does not guarantee that the issue will be resolved within two business days.

## Information Required

When contacting customer support, customers should provide:

- Order number, if applicable
- Email address used during checkout
- Description of the issue
- Tracking number for delivery issues
- Photographs for damaged or incorrect products, when available

Customers should never share passwords, payment-card numbers, security codes, or account verification codes.

## Shipping Support

Customers should contact support if an order has not arrived **2 business days after its estimated delivery date**.

## Tracking Support

Customers should contact support when:

- Tracking has not updated for 3 business days.
- A package remains missing 24 hours after being marked delivered.
- Tracking information cannot be found after shipment.

## Return Support

Customers can request a return within **30 calendar days after delivery**.

Customer support reviews eligibility and provides return instructions.

## Refund Support

Customers may contact support with their order number to request information about their refund.

Approved refunds are processed within **5–7 business days after approval**.

## Escalation Procedure

If the initial support response does not resolve a customer's problem, the customer may request escalation.

Escalation requests should be submitted in the existing support conversation.

The support team aims to respond to escalated cases within **3 business days**.

Escalation does not guarantee a particular outcome.

## Unsupported Questions

If the chatbot cannot find information in the knowledge base, it should explain that the information is unavailable.

It should direct customers to customer support for clarification.

The chatbot must not invent store policies or make promises that are not supported by the documentation.

## Chatbot Restrictions

The chatbot cannot:

- Access real customer accounts
- Modify orders
- Change shipping addresses
- Process refunds
- Approve returns
- Access payment information
- Retrieve real-time shipping information

The chatbot provides information based only on the available knowledge-base documents.
