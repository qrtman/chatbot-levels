Order Tracking Policy

Demo Store provides order tracking information for dispatched orders.

How to Track an Order

Customers can track their orders using either of the following methods:

Open the shipping confirmation email and click the tracking link.

Sign in to their Demo Store account and navigate to Account → Orders → Track Package.

Tracking Number Availability

Tracking numbers become available after an order has been dispatched.

Customers receive an order confirmation email after placing an order.

A separate shipping confirmation email is sent when the order ships.

Tracking Activation

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to become active.

A tracking number without updates does not necessarily indicate that a package is lost.

Tracking Statuses

Label Created

A shipping label has been generated, but the carrier may not have received or scanned the package.

In Transit

The carrier has received the package and is transporting it toward its destination.

Out for Delivery

The carrier expects to attempt delivery that day.

Delivered

The carrier has marked the package as delivered.

This status does not confirm who received the package.

Tracking Not Updating

If tracking information has not updated for 3 business days after the last carrier scan, customers should contact customer support.

Customers should provide their order number and tracking number.

Package Marked Delivered but Missing

If an order is marked as delivered but cannot be found:

Check the delivery location.

Check nearby safe locations.

Ask household members whether they received the package.

Wait 24 hours after the delivery scan.

Contact customer support if the package is still missing.

Late Deliveries

Customers should contact support if their package has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date.

Tracking Limitations

The chatbot cannot access real-time carrier tracking systems.

It must not invent tracking numbers, package locations, delivery dates, or delivery confirmations.
