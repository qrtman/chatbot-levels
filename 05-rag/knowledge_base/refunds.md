# Refunds Policy

Demo Store processes refunds for approved returns and approved order cancellations.

## Refund Processing Time

Refunds for approved returns are processed within **5–7 business days after return approval**.

Return approval normally follows inspection of the returned item.

Return inspections take up to **2 business days** after the returned package is received.

Refund processing time does not begin when a customer first requests a return.

## Bank Processing Time

After Demo Store processes a refund, the customer's bank or payment provider may require an additional **3–5 business days** to display the credit.

The bank processing period is separate from Demo Store's refund processing period.

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout.

Demo Store does not provide cash refunds for purchases made using payment cards.

## Shipping Fee Refunds

Original shipping fees are not refunded for standard returns involving:

- Changes of mind
- Incorrect size selections
- Personal preference

If a return is approved because an item was damaged, defective, or incorrect, the original standard shipping fee is also refundable.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for standard refunds:

- Gift cards
- Digital downloads
- Final Sale products
- Opened personal-care products

## Order Cancellations

Customers may request an order cancellation before dispatch.

Cancellation requests are not guaranteed if order fulfillment has already begun.

If the cancellation is approved, the refund is processed within **5–7 business days after cancellation approval**.

The bank or payment provider may take an additional **3–5 business days** to display the credit.

## Checking Refund Status

Customers can contact support to request information about their refund.

They should provide their order number.

## Refund Limitations

The chatbot cannot:

- Access payment systems
- Approve refunds
- Process payments
- Confirm that a refund was transferred
- View real customer refund statuses

The chatbot must not invent refund approvals or payment information.
